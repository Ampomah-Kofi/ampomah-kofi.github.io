Adversarial Transferability in Medical Imaging

Attack Success Rate is Not All You Need

A systematic study across 4 medical imaging datasets, 7 models spanning CNNs and ViTs, 7 attacks, and 5 perturbation budgets, showing that Attack Success Rate alone is an inadequate measure of adversarial robustness in medical AI.

  • Multi-metric evaluation (ASR, SSIM, PSNR, L2) across 3,500 attack configurations
  • Finds ASR is decoupled from perceptual quality and perturbation strength across CNNs and ViTs
  • Cross-architecture transferability analysis: ViTs transfer uniformly, CNNs vary by model
