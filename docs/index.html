Security Focus

Security & Privacy Focus

What I bring to a security & privacy internship — where my research, coursework, and tooling meet real-world defense.

I study how security and privacy fail for real people — from adversarial attacks that transfer across ML models to opaque data collection by third-party bots — and I build the measurement, auditing, and human-centered tooling that surfaces those risks and makes systems more accountable. I pair peer-reviewed research with hands-on defensive skills (SIEM, network security, incident response) from the Google Cybersecurity program.

Adversarial ML & Robustness

Crafting and evaluating adversarial attacks (FGSM/PGD) and measuring transferability across model architectures.

Usable Privacy Auditing

Detection & Monitoring
